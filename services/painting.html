Our Process

01

Free Estimate

We measure every surface, note existing damage, and give you a transparent, itemized quote.

02

Prep Work

We patch holes, sand rough spots, caulk gaps, mask surfaces, and prime where needed before any paint touches a wall.

03

Paint & Finish

Two coats of premium paint applied cleanly with professional tools — rollers, brushes, and sprayers as needed.

04

Cleanup

We remove all tape, clean all surfaces, and do a final walkthrough to make sure every edge is perfect.
