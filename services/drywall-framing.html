How It Works

Our Process

01

Site Assessment

We evaluate existing structures, check for moisture/mold, and measure the area before any work starts.

02

Framing

Steel or wood framing is installed to code, with proper blocking for electrical, plumbing, and insulation.

03

Drywall Hang & Finish

Sheets are hung, taped, mudded, and sanded to a smooth, paint-ready finish with your chosen texture.

04

Prime Ready

We prime the finished surfaces and leave everything clean and ready for your painter — or we can paint too.
