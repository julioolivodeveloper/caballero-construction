Kitchen Remodel Cost in Riverside, CA:
2024 Complete Guide

June 2024 7 min read Riverside, CA

A kitchen remodel is one of the most impactful — and most searched — home improvements for Riverside County homeowners. But costs can vary wildly depending on who you ask. This guide cuts through the noise with real, local price ranges based on projects we've completed across Riverside, Corona, Jurupa Valley, Moreno Valley, and Fontana.

The biggest variable is scope. Here's how Riverside-area kitchens typically break down by investment level:

Inland Empire vs. National Averages

National averages often cite $25,000–$75,000. In Riverside, CA, labor costs are higher than the US average but lower than Los Angeles. Expect to pay roughly 15–20% more than Midwest pricing but 10–20% less than central LA.

What Drives the Cost of a Kitchen Remodel

1. Cabinets (30–40% of total budget)

Cabinets are the single biggest cost factor in any kitchen remodel. Your options:

  • Stock cabinets — Pre-made, fastest lead time, lowest cost ($80–$200 per linear foot installed). Limited sizes and styles. Good for tight budgets or rentals.
  • Semi-custom cabinets — Built to order in standard sizes with more finish options ($150–$400 per linear foot installed). The sweet spot for most Riverside homeowners.
  • Full custom cabinets — Any size, any material, any finish ($400–$1,200+ per linear foot). Worth it for unusual layouts or luxury builds.

2. Countertops (10–15% of total budget)

Quartz and granite remain the most popular choices in the Inland Empire, and for good reason — they handle the region's heat well and require minimal maintenance. Current pricing in Riverside:

  • Laminate: $15–$30/sq ft installed — budget-friendly, decent look
  • Granite: $50–$100/sq ft installed — classic, durable, each slab is unique
  • Quartz: $60–$120/sq ft installed — most popular right now, non-porous, low maintenance
  • Quartzite/Marble: $80–$200+/sq ft — premium, stunning, requires sealing

3. Layout Changes (Major Cost Multiplier)

Moving a sink, refrigerator, or stove involves rerouting plumbing and/or electrical — which adds $1,500–$8,000+ to any project. If you can keep appliances and the sink in the same location, you'll save significantly.

4. Appliances (10–20% of total budget)

We often get asked whether appliances should be included in the contractor quote. Short answer: they usually aren't. Appliances are typically purchased separately and we handle the installation. Budget $3,000–$8,000 for mid-range appliances and $10,000+ for professional-grade.

Is a Kitchen Remodel Worth It in the Inland Empire?

In a word: yes. The Inland Empire real estate market has been strong, and kitchen remodels consistently deliver one of the best returns on investment of any home improvement. A mid-range kitchen remodel typically returns 60–80% of its cost at resale — and more importantly, it dramatically improves your quality of life while you're living there.

Buyers touring homes in Riverside, Corona, and Moreno Valley increasingly prioritize kitchen condition. An outdated kitchen can reduce your home's appeal and listing price far more than the cost of a reasonable remodel.

How to Keep Costs Under Control

  • Keep the same layout. The most expensive change you can make is moving major appliances. If your kitchen flows reasonably well, work with what you have.
  • Reface instead of replace cabinets. If your cabinet boxes are structurally sound, refacing the doors and painting the frames can save 30–50% vs full replacement.
  • Buy appliances separately. Shop sales events (Black Friday, Memorial Day) and buy appliances independently rather than letting the contractor source them.
  • Phase the project. If the full remodel is out of reach, start with countertops and hardware this year, cabinets next year.
  • Get multiple quotes — but not too many. Get 3 quotes from licensed contractors. More than that becomes time-consuming and confusing.
Pro Tip from Our Team

Always get your permit pulled for work that involves plumbing or electrical changes. In Riverside County, unpermitted work can come back to haunt you at resale — and it can be a safety risk. We handle permits for all applicable work.

Timeline: How Long Does a Kitchen Remodel Take?

This is one of the most common questions we hear. Here's a realistic timeline for a mid-range kitchen remodel in Riverside:

  • Planning & permitting: 2–4 weeks
  • Cabinet lead time (semi-custom): 4–8 weeks
  • Demo & rough-in (plumbing/electrical): 3–5 days
  • Cabinet & countertop installation: 5–7 days
  • Tile, flooring, painting, finishing: 5–8 days

Total realistic timeline: 8–14 weeks from first call to finished kitchen. Anyone promising a full custom remodel in 2 weeks is either cutting corners or hasn't accounted for lead times.
